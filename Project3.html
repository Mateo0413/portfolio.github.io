Location: Lubbock, Tx and Bizbee, Az

DATE:Spring 2021

DETAIL OF COURSE

In this course, we were to come up with rib grids for 2 locations. The first location is a given mine and the other location is Lubbock Texas. When the ribbed grid is complete one is to merge the rib-grid to create a site to design a drive-in theater. This drive-in theater needs to have an archive building, projector room, and a large screen for the drive-in theater.

Site Context

The site is not a real location that one can physically go to. It is a combination of 2 different locations to create this ribbed site.



For the design of all my buildings, I decided to continue with the theme of the site. The archive building is constructed in a cage-like structure where one can go up and down the stairs to access the archived material. The projection room and the large screen also follow this design concept to make it feel cage like yet open to the exterior due to the rib-grid theme.



The rib-grid of the mine make the site feel exptremely vast and open.
