DETAIL OF COURSE

In this course, we were to research thresholds found around cities from different locations around the world. With researched thresholds one is to make a theater for SXSW events located in Austin Texas

Site Context

The site is located near Downtown Austin, Tx next to a river. It is surrounded by many bars, a police station, and a couple of parking garages.



When researching thresholds, I decided to go with linear thresholds found around cities. Some of the repeating patters in linear thresholds like streets are that the streets can go on for a couple of miles and then be cut off by a building or park and continue still on the same axis on the other side of the building/park.
